06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A few answers before we start. Have something else in mind?

Ask us on WhatsApp
How long does website development take?

A focused business website often takes around 1–2 weeks once the content is ready. More advanced websites may take 3–6 weeks or longer. We confirm your timeline after reviewing the scope; content readiness and revisions can affect delivery.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo if you have one, service or product details, contact information, written content and any images you want to use. Share your goals and a few reference websites too. If you’re still gathering content, we’ll help you plan what’s needed.

Can you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We can guide you in choosing a domain and suitable hosting, then help with setup. Any registration or hosting fees are agreed with you in advance, and your domain and accounts should remain under your ownership.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance can cover content updates, fixes, backups or software updates where applicable. We’ll agree the tasks, frequency and cost separately so you know what is included after launch.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive design is part of every package. We check layouts and interactions on phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes before launch.

Is SEO included?

We include the agreed SEO foundation, such as page titles, descriptions, heading structure, search-friendly content organization and performance checks. More extensive SEO work can be scoped separately. Search rankings depend on many factors and aren’t guaranteed.

Can you build an e-commerce website?

Yes. We can scope a store with product listings, a shopping cart, checkout and an appropriate payment integration. Requirements such as inventory, delivery, product variants and payment-provider fees are discussed before quoting.

How do revisions work?

We include a review and refinement stage. The number of revision rounds and what they cover are agreed in your project scope. Additional features or a change of direction may require an updated quote or timeline.

What is the payment structure?

We agree the deposit, project milestones and final balance with you before work begins. You’ll receive a clear scope and quote first, so you understand the deliverables and payment schedule.
