What happens when you use the form?

The project form prepares a message in your browser using the details you enter. It does not automatically submit your details to King’s Web Studio or store them in a website database.

You can review the message, edit your details or copy it. If you choose WhatsApp or email, your message is passed to that service or your email app. It reaches us when you send it there.

What information should you share?

Share the contact details and project information needed for us to discuss your website. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information in your inquiry.

How are inquiries used?

Information you send to us is used to respond to your inquiry, understand your requirements and communicate about the project. Contact us if you want us to update or remove information from our project conversations, subject to any records we need to retain for completed work.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts or application cookies. Fonts and images are served with the website. The hosting provider may process basic request information to serve the site. WhatsApp and your email provider have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use those services.
